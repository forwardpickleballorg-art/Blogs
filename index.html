The DIVE Method

Every lesson and clinic runs through the same four-part cycle.

Drilling

Purposeful reps built around the specific shot or pattern you're working on.

Internalizing

Slowing down long enough for the movement to become second nature, not just understood.

Validating

Testing the skill under real point pressure to confirm it holds up.

Enjoying

Keeping the process fun — progress sticks best in a community that enjoys the grind together.
